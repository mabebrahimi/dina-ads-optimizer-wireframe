How Dina Works
Learn how the system analyzes your Google Ads account and identifies opportunities.
Data Ingestion
Dina connects to your Google Ads account and retrieves performance data across campaigns, ad groups, keywords, and search terms. All data is normalized and preserved for evidence views.
Waste Detection
Identifies wasted spend by root cause: research intent traffic, broad match expansion, bad geography, schedule inefficiency, and device mismatches. Always includes campaign and ad group source.
Tracking Audit
Validates conversion tracking integrity. Detects broken or suspicious conversion actions, double counting, missing call tracking, and click/session mismatches.
Opportunity Detection
Finds scalable growth opportunities by identifying high-performing queries and segments. Detects limiting factors like budget caps, impression share loss, and rank limitations.
Insight Prioritization
Ranks findings by estimated impact, confidence level, and risk. Classifies insights into Waste, Tracking, and Opportunity categories for clear decision routing.
Action Plans
Converts insights into executable tasks with clear scope (account, campaign, ad group), expected impact, and required permissions. All actions are auditable.
